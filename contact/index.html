Phone: (425) 592-3399, open 24/7. Describe what happened, whether water is still moving, and where you are. Dispatch explains the next step and the scope and price before work starts.

This site takes calls only. There is no contact form, so a phone call is the quickest route to a crew.

Business details

Mukilteo Water Damage Pros, 6500 Harbour Heights Pkwy, Mukilteo, WA 98275. Email: contact@mukilteowaterdamagepros.com. Email is checked during business hours, so call for anything urgent.

Before you call

If water is near electricity or you smell gas, call 911 or your utility first. Then call us.

What to have ready

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from, and whether you stopped it.
  • How much of the building is wet.
  • Your insurer's name, if you plan to file a claim.
