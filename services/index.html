Which service do you need first?

Most losses move through the same order. Water removal and mitigation come first, because they stop the damage. Cleanup comes next, repair after that, and mold remediation only if moisture left growth behind.

Situation to starting point
What you are seeingStart with
Water on the floor right nowEmergency water removal
Water removed but walls and floors still wetWater mitigation
Debris, odors or ruined materials after the waterWater damage cleanup
Storm water, groundwater or sewageFlood damage restoration

If you are not sure, call. Dispatch asks a few questions and points you to the right step, and whole-job water damage restoration is covered on the home page.

Safety steps before you call

  • Stay out of rooms where water is near outlets, and leave the building if you smell gas.
  • Shut off the water at the main valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Do not walk through standing water that may be contaminated.
  • Take photos before anything is moved.

How the services fit together

A single loss often uses four of the core services in a row. A burst pipe, for example, starts with water removal, moves to mitigation, then cleanup, then repair. Mold remediation joins only if moisture stayed long enough for growth.
