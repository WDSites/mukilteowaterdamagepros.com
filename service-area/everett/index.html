When water is spreading in Everett

A burst supply line, a failed water heater or a backed-up drain does not wait for business hours. Every hour water sits in drywall, subfloor and insulation, the job gets bigger. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for Everett addresses, and the crew works from Mukilteo, just across Japanese Gulch from the Everett line.

Same-day response when available is the standard we work toward, and the person who answers will ask what the water is doing, where it came from and whether anyone is near electrical equipment. Those answers decide which equipment goes on the truck first.

If water is still spreading, do this first

Stop the source

Shut off the fixture valve or the main water valve. If you cannot find it, leave the area and call your water utility.

Power only if it is safe

Cut power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel while standing dry. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and call the utility or 911 first.

Lift what you can

Move valuables, electronics and small furniture onto dry counters or upper floors. Pull rugs and wet cushions away from the water.

Keep people and pets out

Sewage or floodwater can carry contamination. Keep children and pets out of standing water, then call (425) 592-3399.

What the crew does after you call

The first job is to stop more damage, which is the core of water mitigation. The crew confirms the water is shut off, checks where it traveled and maps moisture in walls and floors with meters and thermal cameras. Scope and price explained before work starts, so you know what is planned before equipment goes down.

Next comes emergency water removal, where pumps and extraction units pull standing water out of the structure. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers then dry what is left. Drying typically takes a few days, and it depends on materials and conditions.

Drywall and insulation that stayed wet are often removed rather than dried, and that is part of water damage cleanup. Once the structure reads dry, water damage repair covers drywall, flooring, paint and cabinets. If the water came from a storm, groundwater or sewage, flood damage restoration applies instead.

Safety before the phone call

If water is touching outlets, a panel or any appliance, or you smell gas, leave the area. Call the utility or 911 before calling the restoration line. Never wade through water that might be sewage.

What affects the cost of water damage work

No honest company can name a number without seeing the water. These are the factors that move the total up or down:

  • Water category: clean supply water costs less to handle than Category 2 or 3 water
  • Area and materials: how many rooms are wet, and whether the floors are carpet, hardwood or tile
  • How long the water sat: older water means more removal and a longer dry-out
  • Equipment and days: the number of air movers and dehumidifiers and how long they run
  • Access and rebuild: whether walls or floors must be opened and then repaired

On insurance, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy, ask your insurer what applies, and photograph the damage when it is safe.
