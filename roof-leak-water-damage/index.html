How rain gets past a Mukilteo roof

A roof rarely fails all at once. Water slips in at the weak points: a cracked pipe boot, a lifted corner of flashing, a valley packed with needles and moss, or the curb of a skylight. Bath fan vents and chimney edges do the same thing over a few wet seasons.

Mukilteo has mild, rainy winters and dry summers, so a small opening can sit unnoticed until the fall storms arrive. The water seldom drips straight down. It runs along a rafter or the top of a wall, soaks the attic insulation, and shows up as a stain several feet from the real entry point.

Safety steps when water comes through the ceiling

  1. If water drips near a light, outlet or the panel, stay out of that room. Cut power only if you can reach the panel while standing on a dry floor.
  2. Call 911 and Snohomish County PUD for sparking, a buzzing fixture or downed lines before you call us.
  3. Smell gas? Leave the house and call 911, then Puget Sound Energy.
  4. Stay out from under a bulging ceiling and keep others away. Do not poke it, and call us so the crew can plan the release.
  5. Do not climb onto a wet roof. A roofer handles access and temporary protection.
  6. Move furniture and electronics out of the drip zone and keep children and pets away.

What the crew does once the water is inside

A roofer fixes the roof. We dry and rebuild the building underneath it. Our crew starts by finding how far the water traveled, using a moisture meter on the ceiling, walls and floor, and a thermal camera to spot wet insulation and framing above the stain.

Wet ceiling drywall and soaked insulation are often removed rather than dried, since they hold water against the framing. Air movers then push dry air across the exposed cavity, and LGR dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room. Extraction units handle any water that reached the floor, and an air scrubber with HEPA filtration helps when demolition stirs up dust.

Ask any company whether its crews follow ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage. Rain through a roof is usually clean when it arrives, but it picks up dust and insulation fibers on the way down. Drying typically takes a few days, and that depends on the materials and conditions.

Why the first fall storms matter most

NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals for Paine Field show October through March adding up to about 22.2 of the roughly 33.4 inches that fall in a year, close to two thirds. That means most of the roof's work happens in six months. Book a roof inspection in September, not after the first soaking.

The same stretch is when wet insulation has the least chance to dry on its own. Cold, damp attics move moisture slowly. If you see a stain in December, assume the cavity above it is wetter than the ceiling looks.

Permits, roofers and the order of work

The City of Mukilteo says all re-roofing projects require a permit, even overlays. Ask your roofer who pulls it and ask the city building department if you are unsure. A permit also means the work should be inspected, which is worth knowing before you agree to a quick patch or a full replacement.

Sequence matters here. The roof opening has to be stopped before drying can hold, or the next storm re-wets everything. Our crew coordinates around the roofer, drying the structure while the roofing work goes on and rebuilding the ceiling once the moisture readings are normal.

Roof leaks and your policy

Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. A storm that lifts shingles is a different story from flashing that wore out over years. Check your policy, and ask your insurer how it treats roof age and wear.

The Washington Insurance Commissioner advises calling your insurer or agent as soon as possible and not making permanent repairs until the insurer has seen the property. Photograph everything and keep receipts. Temporary protection is usually fine, but a full re-roof before the inspection is not. Nobody can promise what a policy will pay.

Moisture, mold and what to do next

According to EPA guidance, water-damaged areas and items should be dried within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold. Wet insulation and drywall are the usual trouble spots, since a hidden attic cavity does not dry in that window on its own. Call early, and if mold shows up afterward, our mold remediation page explains how containment and removal work.

Once the structure is dry, water damage repair covers the rebuild: drywall, insulation, paint and trim. Wind-driven rain through walls is a different loss, covered under storm water damage.

Mistakes that turn a small leak into a big one

  • Waiting for a dry day to look at the ceiling
  • Painting over a stain before the cavity is dry
  • Climbing a wet roof to find the hole
  • Putting a tarp on without a roofer's guidance
  • Throwing out wet materials before photographing them
  • Letting a roofer close the ceiling before it is dry
