Privacy Policy

This site is call-only. Here is what happens to the information you share when you call or visit.

☎ Call (425) 592-3399

What we collect

When you call, dispatch may ask for your name, address, phone number and a description of the damage so a crew can respond. We use that information to answer your call, connect you with a restoration professional, and follow up on your request.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This website

This site has no forms and no accounts. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map, so Google may receive your IP address and browser details when those load. Your browser settings control cookies.

How information is shared

Call details are shared with the restoration professionals who respond to your request, and with service providers who help us run the phone line. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

If you would like us to delete information from a past call, contact us by phone or at contact@mukilteowaterdamagepros.com. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.

Our service area

Dispatch covers Mukilteo and the nearby towns of Everett, Lynnwood and Edmonds in Snohomish County.
